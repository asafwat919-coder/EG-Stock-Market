Nile Trader · iOS

Trade the Egyptian Exchange with an AI co-pilot.

This is a working browser preview of the iOS app. Prices tick live, the AI engine re-scores every stock as they move, and you can place paper trades.

  • Tap any stock to open its chart, AI rating and order book
  • Buy or sell from the detail screen
  • Ask the AI Analyst about a ticker in the Insights tab
  • Switch your risk profile in Profile to see ratings shift

Prices are simulated around indicative levels and are not live EGX quotes. Not affiliated with the Egyptian Exchange. Nothing here is investment advice.
